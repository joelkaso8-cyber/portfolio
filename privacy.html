Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page says, in plain words, what this website collects and what happens to it.

Who is responsible

Joel Kaso, web designer (Albania). Write to me at joelkaso8@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Only what you type into the contact form yourself: your name, your business name (optional), your email and your message.
  • If you write to me on WhatsApp, call me or email me, I receive what you send there.
  • Nothing is collected just because you visit. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Where it goes

  • The form is delivered to my Gmail inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a service that turns forms into emails. Your message passes through their servers.
  • If sending fails, your own email app opens with the message ready, and you send it yourself.
  • The WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp (Meta), where their own policy applies.
  • The chat assistant sends what you type there to Cloudflare (Workers AI) to write the answer. I don't store chats, and Cloudflare doesn't use them to train its models. Please don't share private details in the chat.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every host, GitHub records technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Why I use it

  • To answer your message and prepare a quote.
  • The legal basis is your request (steps before a contract) and my legitimate interest in replying to you.
  • I never sell your details and never use them for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses no cookies. It saves two small things in your own browser only: the language you chose, and the times of the last messages you sent (spam protection). You can clear them at any time in your browser settings. Fonts are stored on this website, so opening a page sends nothing to Google.

How long I keep it

Only as long as needed to handle your request and any work that follows, and no longer than 12 months unless the law requires longer (for example for invoices).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you, or object to how I use it. Write to joelkaso8@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Sites shown in the portfolio

The demo sites and client projects shown in the portfolio have their own privacy pages.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), the form is protected against spam bots, and I keep only what I need.
