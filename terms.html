Terms of service

Last updated: 23 November 2026

These terms explain how a website project with me works. The written quote for your project, once you accept it, sets the exact price, scope and dates; where it differs from this page, the quote applies.

Who I am

Joel Kaso, web designer.
Location: Përmet, Albania.
Email: joelkaso8@gmail.com

What I do

I design and build websites for businesses: the design, the code and the setup, including WhatsApp or booking requests, maps and English/Albanian content where the package includes them.

Quotes and prices

  • The packages on this website (Essential Landing from 150€, Business Pro from 300€, Custom Signature 600€+) are starting prices. Your final price is in the written quote.
  • Work that is not in the accepted quote is quoted separately before I start it.
  • Prices are quoted in EUR and represent the total fee billed by Joel Kaso (no VAT added unless required by applicable law).

Free preview

Before you pay anything, I can show you a free preview of your homepage. You decide afterwards; the preview creates no obligation for either of us.

Payment

  • 50% deposit to start the build, 50% at launch.
  • Before the second payment you check the finished site on a private link on your own phone.

Revisions

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds: 2 for Essential Landing and Business Pro, 3 for Custom Signature. Further changes are quoted separately or covered by the care plan after launch.

Timing and your materials

Delivery times (3–4 days for Essential Landing, 5–10 days for Business Pro, 10–14 days for Custom Signature) start when I have the deposit and the texts, photos and details I need from you. If materials or answers arrive late, the dates move by the same time.

Your content

You confirm that you have the right to use the texts, photos, logos and other material you send me, and that they are accurate. You are responsible for the prices, offers and information shown on your site.

Ownership

  • Once the project is paid in full, the website and its code are yours. I hand over the code, so you can move it or work with another designer.
  • Your domain is registered in your own name and account.
  • I may show the finished site in my portfolio, unless you ask me not to.

Third-party services

Your site may use services run by other companies, such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Maps, the hosting provider, form services or booking tools. Their own terms and prices apply, and I am not responsible for their outages or changes.

Care plan (optional)

After launch you can choose the care plan at 25€ a month: I make your text, price and photo changes, usually within two days, and check every month that the site, the booking and your Google listing work. You can stop it at any time; the site stays yours.

Cancellation and refunds

Either party may cancel before work begins, with a full refund of the deposit. Once design or development has started, the 50% deposit covers the work completed and is non-refundable. If the project is cancelled before launch, you receive all files completed up to that point.

Liability

I build and test every site with care, but I cannot guarantee that a website brings a specific number of bookings or customers. My total liability for any claim related to a project is limited to the total amount paid by the client for that project.

Demo sites

Sites marked Demo in the portfolio are examples with made-up business names. They are not real businesses, and requests sent from them are not real bookings.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Albania. Any dispute is settled by the competent courts of the Republic of Albania.

Contact

Questions about these terms: joelkaso8@gmail.com. How I handle personal data is explained in the privacy policy.
