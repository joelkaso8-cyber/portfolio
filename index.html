          Common questions

          Everything you need to know before we start.

          Are there any monthly fees after the site goes live?

          No monthly lock-in with me. You pay once for the design and build, and the website is 100% yours. Your only ongoing cost is your annual domain name (around €10–15/year).

          How long does it take from start to finish?

          Most websites go live within 1 to 2 weeks. You will see the first working version on your phone within 7 days of our initial call.

          Can I change prices, photos, or menus myself later?

          Yes. When we launch, I send you a short video showing you exactly how to update your text, prices, and photos in minutes—no technical skills needed.

          How do bookings and orders reach me?

          However is easiest for you. Requests can go straight to your WhatsApp pre-filled with the customer's details, to your email, or into a booking calendar.

          How does payment work?

          50% to start the design, and the remaining 50% only when the website is finished, tested, and ready to go live.
