Before you come

Short answers to what guests ask most.

How can I pay?

In cash, in euros or Albanian lek, or by card at reception.

Can I arrive after 21:30?

Yes. Tell us your arrival time in advance by WhatsApp or phone, and we will arrange your check-in.

Can I book a traditional dinner?

Yes. The restaurant cooks traditional dishes from the valley. Ask at reception or send us a WhatsApp message earlier in the day, so the kitchen can prepare.

Are children welcome?

Yes. The family suite has room for parents and children. Ask us about an extra bed or a cot when you book.

Can I bring a pet?

Please ask us before you book, and we will tell you what is possible.

Privacy policy

Hotel Kaso Ervehe, Lagjia Partizani, Përmet 6401, Albania, is responsible for the personal data described here.

What we collect

Only what you give us in a booking request or a message: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, dates, number of guests and the room you ask for. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

To answer your request, confirm and manage your stay, and keep the records the law asks a hotel to keep.

Who sees it

Our staff, and the services that carry your request: WhatsApp, your email provider or, for the form on this site, Telegram (where our staff receive requests) and Google Sheets (where we keep the list of requests). Their own privacy policies apply to that step. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for your stay and for the accounting and guest-registration records required by Albanian law, then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by contacting hotel.ervehe@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Albanian Information and Data Protection Commissioner.

Cookies and storage

The site stores two small settings in your browser: your cookie choice and your language. If you accept, we may later add anonymous visit statistics; if you decline, nothing else is stored. You can change your choice by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Terms of service

Booking requests

A request sent from this site is not yet a booking. Your stay is confirmed only when we reply with the room, the dates and the rate in writing.

Rates

Prices on this site are starting rates per room per night. The rate in our confirmation is the one that applies.

Arrival and departure

Check-in is from 14:00 to 21:30 and check-out is by 11:00. Tell us in advance if you will arrive later.

Changes and cancellations

The cancellation terms are the ones stated in your confirmation. Bookings made through other sites follow that site’s terms.

During your stay

The hotel is smoke-free indoors. Please treat the rooms, the garden and other guests with care.

This website

We keep the information here accurate, but facilities and times can change. Photographs of Përmet are by the photographers credited in the footer and are used under their licences.
