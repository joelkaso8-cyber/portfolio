Nothing touches skin until the stencil is right

A fine-line tattoo is only as good as its placement. So every Bojë piece goes through the same four steps, whether it is a 3 cm date on a wrist or a full back.

  1. 1

    Message us

    Send a WhatsApp with the idea, the placement, a rough size and any reference images. We reply the same day, in English or Albanian.

  2. 2

    Your artist draws it

    The drawing comes back within 1–3 days. A €30 deposit holds your date and comes off the final price. Changes to the drawing are free.

  3. 3

    We fit the stencil

    On the day, the design goes on as a violet thermal stencil. We wipe it off and move it as many times as it takes. You say yes before the needle starts.

  4. 4

    Session & aftercare

    You leave with a protective film, written aftercare in your language, and a free touch-up within three months if anything heals unevenly.

  • Single-use needles and cartridges, opened in front of you
  • Surfaces wrapped and wiped down between every client
  • Vegan inks from EU-compliant suppliers
  • 18+ only, with photo ID
