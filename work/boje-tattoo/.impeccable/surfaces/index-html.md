# Surface: Bojë home page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitors: Tirana locals and visiting diaspora/tourists on phones, arriving from Instagram or Maps. Job: pick an artist by style, see honest price ranges, start a WhatsApp consultation. Proof: process (consultation, drawing, stencil, session), resident artists' work, flash with prices, reviews (synthetic, demo-labelled). Constraints: Joel's CLAUDE.md scroll hero; EN/SQ; demo ribbon; noindex; not in the portfolio grid.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio's own working material, the thermal stencil sheet, is the page. Every piece starts as violet carbon line on translucent tissue, taped down and adjusted before a needle touches skin. The page refuses the category default: a black screen with full-bleed photos, neon-gold CTAs and an Instagram grid.

OWN-WORLD: A cool tissue-grey ground (#ECEBEF). Ink-violet-black (#17151D) for type and the flash wall. Stencil violet (#4C3BC2) for linework, primary actions and active states, with nothing else saturated. Translucent tissue sheets with 1px edges are held by masking-tape tabs and set slightly off-square. Gloock carries the display type and Hanken Grotesk the body. The tissue, the tape and the violet line remain recognisable with all content removed.

STORY: The visitor sees that this is a fine-line and blackwork studio in Blloku. They trust it because it shows the process and real prices, then pick an artist or a flash design and message on WhatsApp, or send the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Process footage plays full-bleed, graded to ink, behind a large taped tissue sheet with violet linework. Scrolling peels the sheet up and away while the film scrubs forward, and reverses when scrolling up. The headline slip is pinned bottom-left (bottom on phones): H1 "Fine-line & blackwork in Blloku, Tirana", one line of copy, a violet **Book a consultation on WhatsApp** button and a secondary "See flash & prices" link. The slip stays readable the whole time. A tissue header with the EN/SQ switch and a WhatsApp CTA sits on top.

FORM: Stencil Sheets, the pick card (Impeccable's own top grounded candidate) chosen over the assigned Placement Atlas. seed:b13f99674d808cd0. Signature interaction: **lift the sheet**. Each artist and flash sheet's tissue lifts on its tape hinge (hover, tap or keyboard) to show the healed photo underneath. Motion grammar: tissue peels and settles with paper-like easing. Reduced motion: sheets stay lifted halfway, nothing is hidden, and the film is a still poster.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
