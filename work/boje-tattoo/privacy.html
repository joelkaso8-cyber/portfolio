Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what this website collects and what happens to it.

Who is responsible

Bojë Tattoo is a demo website made by Joel Kaso. It is not a real tattoo studio. Messages sent from this demo go to Joel Kaso: joelkaso8@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Only what you type into the consultation form yourself: your name, your email or phone number, the artist, date, placement and idea you describe.
  • Nothing is collected just because you visit the page. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request. The legal basis is your request and our legitimate interest in replying to you.
  • We never sell your details and never use them for marketing.

Who else sees it

The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to an email inbox, under FormSubmit's own privacy policy. The WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp, and the map is loaded from OpenStreetMap; their own policies apply there.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies. It saves one small setting in your own browser, your language choice, so the site remembers it. Fonts, photos and the film are served from this website.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to answer your request, and no longer than 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by writing to joelkaso8@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
