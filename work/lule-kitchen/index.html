Since 2016

A family kitchen that moved to London

Lule started as Sunday lunch for friends: one long table, one tray of byrek, too many people. Now it is twelve tables on Green Lanes, and the byrek still comes out of the oven every hour.

Everything is cooked from scratch: the filo is rolled by hand, the yoghurt is strained here, and the grill runs on charcoal.
