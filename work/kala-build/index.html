Questions

Do I need to move out?

For kitchens and bathrooms, almost never. We set up a temporary sink or keep one bathroom working. For whole-floor work we plan it with you.

Are you insured?

Yes: £5m public liability. Gas work is done by Gas Safe engineers and electrics are signed off by a NICEIC electrician.

How do payments work?

A 10% deposit to book, stage payments as work is finished, and the last 10% only after you sign off.

Can you work with my own designer?

Of course. Send us the drawings and we quote to them.
