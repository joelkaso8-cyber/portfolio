    04 · Brewing guide

    A good filter cup in three steps

    1. Grind

      15 g of coffee, medium-fine, like coarse sand.

    2. Bloom

      Pour 30 g of water just off the boil and wait 30 seconds.

    3. Pour

      Pour slowly to 250 g in circles. Done in about 3 minutes.
