06 · Good to know

Never rafted? Here are straight answers.

Most of our guests have never been in a raft before. Here is how it works.

Do I need rafting experience?

No experience needed. The Vjosa route is Class II, making it beginner-friendly and safe for first-timers and families with children.

Do I need to know how to swim?

Swimming is recommended but not required. You wear a certified life jacket the entire time, which keeps you floating safely face up. Your guide explains safety before anyone enters the raft.

What is included in the €30 tour?

English-speaking certified guide, helmet, life jacket, wetsuit, windbreaker jacket, full safety briefing, transport from the meeting point, drinking water, and complimentary trip photos and videos.

What should I bring with me?

A swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, water shoes or secure sandals, and dry clothes to change into after the tour.

Can children join the tour?

Yes. Children from age 3 and up are welcome. We have kid-sized safety gear and the Class II rapids are gentle enough for young adventurers.

How does payment work?

Payment is made in cash on site in Përmet on the day of the tour (€30 or 3,000 ALL per person). No credit card or upfront deposit required.

Who will guide us?

Adion, our founder and lead guide, has over 6 years of guiding experience on the Vjosa River. He speaks fluent English and Albanian and knows every curve of the canyon.
