Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what information Vjosa Rafting Getaway collects through this website and what happens to it.

Who is responsible

Vjosa Rafting Getaway. You can reach us at vjosaraftinggetaway@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Only what you type into a form yourself: for example your name, phone number or email, and the details of your booking or question.
  • Nothing is collected just because you visit the page. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics that follow you.

Why we use it

  • To answer your message, confirm your booking or order, and contact you about it.
  • The legal basis is your request (taking steps before a contract) and our legitimate interest in replying to you.
  • We never sell your details and never use them for marketing unless you clearly agree.

Who else sees it

Your message reaches us through email, WhatsApp or a booking service, which handle it under their own privacy policies. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps or other sites open those services, and their own policies apply there.

Cookies and storage

This site uses no tracking cookies. It may save small settings in your own browser, such as your language choice, so the site remembers them. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. Fonts are stored on this website, so loading a page does not send your details to Google.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to handle your request and any booking that follows, and no longer than 12 months unless the law requires longer (for example for invoices).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Just contact us at vjosaraftinggetaway@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), forms are protected against spam bots, and we only keep what we need.
