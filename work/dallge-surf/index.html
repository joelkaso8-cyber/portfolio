Questions

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

You need to be comfortable in the sea. Beginner lessons stay in waist-deep water between the lifeguard flags.

What if the waves are too big or flat?

We check at 7 a.m. If it isn't safe or fun, we move you to another day or refund in full.

From what age?

Kids' lessons from 7. Under-16s in adult groups need a parent in the water too.

Where do we meet?

The blue hut at the north end of Fistral Beach, 15 minutes before your lesson. Parking is above the beach.
