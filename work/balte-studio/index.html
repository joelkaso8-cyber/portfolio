    Day 14. We message you on WhatsApp when it is ready. Safe for food and for the dishwasher.

I still have the first bowl I ever made. It is terrible.

I was an accountant for eleven years. In 2015 a friend dragged me to a pottery class in Thessaloniki, and for two hours I did not think about a single spreadsheet. A year later I rented an old shoemaker's shop behind Pazari i Ri and put in three wheels. We have nine now.

I still teach most of the beginner classes myself, with Genti and Sara. None of us will take the clay out of your hands to "fix" your pot. A cup that leans a little and is yours beats a perfect one that is ours.

Your first piece will come out crooked. You will love it anyway.

Mira Dushkufounder and teacher
