06 · Questions

Before you book.

Still unsure about something? Ask me on Instagram. No question is silly.

Does it hurt?

A little, and it depends on the spot. Ribs and feet feel more, arms and thighs less. We stop for breaks whenever you want.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the size and detail. Send me your idea and I'll give you a clear price before you book.

I only have a rough idea. Is that okay?

Yes. Most people start that way. Send what you have and we'll shape it together.

Do you tattoo in color?

No. I work only in black. That's my style, and it's why the work looks the way it does.

It's my first tattoo. What should I know?

Tell me it's your first. I'll explain every step before we start. Eat well, sleep well and come as you are.

Can you cover an old tattoo?

Often, yes. Black is strong for cover-ups. Send me a photo of the old tattoo and I'll tell you honestly.

How long does healing take?

The surface heals in about two to four weeks. I'll give you simple aftercare steps to follow.
