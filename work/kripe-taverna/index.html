Kripë means salt

The house way: the whole fish baked under a crust of sea salt from the Narta pans, cracked open at your table. It keeps every bit of moisture in, and nothing else goes on it but lemon and olive oil from the hills above Himarë.

  • Në skarë

    Over olive-wood coals, with oregano picked on the hill. The way most people want it.

  • E skuqur

    For the small ones: red mullet and squid, dusted in flour and fried in a minute.

  • Në tavë

    Octopus and the day’s soup go in the clay pot with onion, tomato and a glass of white.
